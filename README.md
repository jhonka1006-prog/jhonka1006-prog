## About Me

I work at the intersection of research, public policy, technology, and high-performance sport. My approach is grounded in evidence, discipline, and long-term thinking, applied equally to scientific inquiry, software systems, and athletic performance.

- **Researcher** — designing and executing evidence-based studies in economics, public policy, and sports science.
- **Politician** — advocating for accessible, transparent, and evidence-informed public institutions.
- **Economics Student** — studying markets, incentives, and policy design to inform sound decision-making.
- **Software Developer** — building reliable software, APIs, and automation for research and public impact.
- **Para Athlete** — training toward elite international competition with a data-driven performance methodology.
- **Open Source Contributor** — maintaining and contributing to tools that create shared, long-term value.
- **Artificial Intelligence Enthusiast** — applying AI and data science to research, governance, and sport.
